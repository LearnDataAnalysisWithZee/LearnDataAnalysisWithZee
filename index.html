Data Analysis Ladder

Data analysis ladder is a three step system that is used to increase insight by extracting information from data, usually pertaining to real world requirements.

  • Exploratory Analytics: Encapsulates experiences found from unstructured data and defines into a question.
  • Descriptive & Diagnostic Analytics: Actionable insight for a design blueprint for an solution that resolves what the problem (Question) was and why the problem was there, preparing the solution for an official release.
  • Prescriptive Analytics: What now extends the solution & paves a way for post-deployment auditing & governance.

Question Defined

What steps will the business take to provide a positive customer experience during the quotation process?

Actionable Insight

Quote Process Strategy

Agent A (Operational Phase)

  • Create customer profile & note their requirements.
  • Mail/share to/with agent B.

Usually administrative tasks.

Agent B (Operational & Commercial Phase)

Negotiate with stakeholders:

  • Customers directly to deliver them their quote price.
  • Customers via third-party companies who own/share risk the product/as far as the product is concerned.
  • Mail/share to/with agent A.

Usually sales teams/agentic AI for sales.

Agent A (Storage & Presentation)

  • Stores customer profile, requirements information alongside the sales outcome and the highlights of the interaction between agent and customer across various reports.
  • Mail/share to/with team leads/directors.

What Now

Agent toolbox (Snapshots of Templates):

The agent toolbox is a repository where templates of conversation scripts for each product are stored for easy access during live interactions with customers.

Conversation script contents:

  • Greetings
  • Introductions based on direction
  • Product specifications
  • What next
  • Conclusions

Artefacts Library (Snapshots of Interactions):

The collection of customer-agent interactions that can act as a data source for various reports and the record-keeping can be used as a testing environment for improvement of conversation scripts for each product.

Stakeholder query reports:

  1. Customer query report: client details, client query and resolution.
  2. Company query report usually consisting of company details, company query and resolution.

Anomalies Detected Report:

Unusual events during operational phase or before pricing event or commercial outcome have been reached, during the quote process that require corrective action usually consisting of customer/contact details, anomaly detected and resolution.

Outliers Flagged Report:

  1. Review historical fact find data.
  2. Investigate causes/contributing reasons to profile risk.
  3. Train agents using guidance achieved to make informed decisions in the future.
  4. Usually consisting of client details, outlier flagged, investigation and outlier status.

Quotes Today Report:

Combines operational and commercial outcomes of each quote, every working day, usually consisting of client details, product type, agents note and the pricing event.

Advertisement Channels Report:

Significant product route from business to customer is through advertisement, the report generally consists of client details, product type, ad channel, conversion status, review (if converted for customer sentiment).
