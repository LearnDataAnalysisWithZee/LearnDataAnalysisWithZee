What steps will the business take to provide a positive customer experience during the quotation process?

Quote process strategy (experience, experimentation & evolution):

Agent A (Operational Phase):

  • Create customer profile & note their requirements
  • Mail/share to/with Agent B

Usually administrative tasks

Agent B (Operational & Commercial Phase):

Negotiate with stakeholders:

  • Customers directly to deliver them their quote price
  • Customers via third-party companies who own/share risk the product as far as the product is concerned
  • Mail/share to/with Agent A

Usually sales teams/agentic AI for sales

Agent A (Storage & Presentation):

  • Stores customer profile, requirements information alongside the sales outcome and highlights of the interaction between agent and customer across various reports.
  • Mail/share to/with team leads/directors
  • Agent toolbox (snapshots of templates) for conversational fact finding during live interactions.
  • Conversational fact finds library (snapshots of interactions):
    1. Conversation introduction scripts based on direction.
    2. Conversational style fact-find questions for customer profiling and requirements.
    3. What happens next as per quote process strategy.
    4. Delivery of pricing event conversation scripts.
  • Stakeholder query reports:
    1. Customer query consisting of client details, query and resolution.
    2. Company query consisting of company details, query and resolution.
  • Anomalies detected report: Unusual events during the quote process that require corrective action, usually including customer/contact details, anomaly detected and resolution.
  • Outliers flagged report:
    1. Review historical fact-find data.
    2. Investigate causes and contributing reasons to profile risk.
    3. Train agents using lessons learned.
    4. Record client details, outlier flagged, investigation and status.
  • Quotes today report: Combines operational and commercial outcomes of each quote, usually including client details, product type, agent notes and pricing event.
  • Advertisement channels report: Includes client details, product type, advertisement channel, conversion status and customer review if converted.

Additional Notes

Researching reports: Make mappings between reports across the business timeline.

Governance for cloud based operating model: Business account.

Ethics for AI use: Creativity, prompting Copilot programs initially and agentic AI workflows.
