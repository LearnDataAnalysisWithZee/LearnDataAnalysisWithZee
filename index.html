Purpose Of Data Analysis

To extract information from data to gain actionable insight for in order to make informed decisions.

Quantitative & Qualitative Data

Quantitative data & descriptive labels = To extract information previously hidden, for informed decision making.

Qualitative data = Information always available that can add to the presentation (Actionable insight) the reasons for each decision.

Capture Data From Relational DB Into Datasets

  • Central process that operational & commercial objectives are neccesarily pivoting around, often quotes, could also be sales, depending on type of business.
  • Preceded by stakeholder queries capturing the movement of each product from business to customer based on interactions between various stakeholders involved.
  • Followed by operational anomalies that captures issues while process (e.g. quotes/sales) in transit.
  • KPI dashboard reporting for tracking positive states product experiences.
  • Investigation of commercial outliers where there exists much distance that could be measured between monetary amounts specific product is sold at.
  • Ongoing investigation of commercial outliers where product initially sold might have to be returned by customer.
