Helpful Workflows

There can be 3 types of workflows:

  • Workflows for compiling reports
  • Workflows for researching reports
  • Workflows for profiling products

Workflows For Compiling Reports

Qoute process strategy workflow:

  • Customer-Agent A interaction
  • Agent A opens Agent Toolbox for conversational fact find selection for specific product
  • Agent A fetches request into fact find template
  • Agent A then submits entry into advertisement channel report & advertisement channel list
  • Agent A Shares/Mails Customer Fact Find With/To Agent B
  • Agent B Prepares Commercial Outcome & Negotiates Pricing With Customer
  • Agent B shares/mails updated customer fact find with/to Agent A
  • If the new/existing customer is quoted, quoted for membership renewal, renews membership or purchases product then the KPIs status reflects that & is fetched into an KPI list
  • If an anomaly arises whereby before reaching the commercial outcome during the quote process there is a waiting time then submit entry into the anomaly detected report & if is taking even longer to be resolved then fetch into the report reminder list also
  • Scan for outliers detected or flagged as per the commercial training section on researching reports & submit entry into their respective reports

Workflow for stakeholder queries:

  • Customer-Agent/Internal Agent-Third Party Agent interaction
  • Submit entry into either customer query or company query report
  • Record resolution and keep updating as per requirement
  • Submit entry into report reminder list if query has not reached resolution point within 24 hours

Workflow for anomalies detected:

  • Customer-agent/internal agent-third party agent interaction or search for anomalies detected from quotes today report
  • Submit entry into anomaly detected report
  • Record resolution and keep updating as per requirement
  • Submit entry into report reminder list if query has not reached resolution point within 24 hours

Workflows For Researching Reports

Workflow for anomalies detected as contained in stakeholder queries or the quotes today reports:

  • Extract anomaly detected entry
  • Read resolution for steps to improve the resolution ladder
  • For anomalies under resolved, escalate to report reminder status and set agent objective to improve resolution for future detections
  • Highlight company-wide rules for newer updates on them
  • Search for finer subtleties contained in the resolution segments of each entry to try and gather means or mechanisms to improve conversation scripts

Workflow for outliers flagged

  • Extract an outlier that is flagged for review from the outliers flagged list
  • An outlier flagged entry will always be as a result of comparing a low priced KPI with a high priced KPI where the product/product type and stakeholders involved are the same per working day
  • Search for contact details for the extracted entry from the list, the contact details can be found in the quotes today report
  • From the fact-find containing details of the two customers involved per outlier and their requirements, select possible reasons that are common in both of them and compare their values to try and narrow down the reasons why such a gap in pricing outcome has occured

Workflow for outliers detected

  • Extract an outlier detected from the outliers detected list
  • An outlier detected entry will always be as a result of an post-operational and commercial outcome where the customer requests to either return/exchange product, adjust/cancel membership or maybe even being declined sale as the ultimate outcome
  • Outliers detected therefore are categorised into return, exchange, adjustment, cancellation or decline
  • There may be various reasons for outliers detected that can be found from multiple sources
  • Most common reasons being, the latest customer-agent interaction whereby customer reveals themselves why they are no longer interested or want to change product, customer-agent interaction for adjusting or cancelling membership or internal-agent liasoning with company-agent{s} or accessing records from third-party companies invoved for reason{s} why sale was declined

Workflows For Product Risk Profiling

Availibility related risks:

  • Source: anomalies detected reports
  • Ongoing investigation: what services/products are currently unavailable yet most desired by customers?
  • Predictions of potential future outcomes/prospects: how likely is the unavailable service/product to be requested by customers in the future based on past data?
  • Recommendations for agent objectives: what steps could be taken to satisfy customers in the meanwhile their desired services/products are unavailable?

Advertisement related risks:

  • Sources: advertisement channels report & list, customer-agent interactions fact finds, quotes today reports, stakeholder queries reports, anomalies detected reports, outliers flagged lists & outliers detected lists
  • Ongoing investigation: for what services/products exactly lead generation by specific advertisement channels scales high yet the conversion success rate is low?
  • Predictions of potential future outcomes/prospects: what could be the causes for advertisement channels not managing to convert leads generated?
  • Recommendations for agent objectives: how do the findings from the many data sources relate to provide insights to re-consider future involvement of advertisement channels in advertising services/products?
