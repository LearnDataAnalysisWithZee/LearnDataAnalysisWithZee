With the idea well underway and an conversation script for each product offered now created in the agent toolbox, one cannot fail to miss how each script well organised as per product, but how will content that is upto standards fill these conversation scripts? The client query reports are a set of reports that encapsulate day-to-day customer-agent interactions and serves as an excellent corpus for the conversation scripts to consume however what we precisely select from these collections of reports needs to be in accordance with general conversational rules while being compatible with the product specifications, in the context of, 'quoting customers on products offered by a business that they (the customer) have developed an interest in'. General conversational rules are that there is usually a greeting, introductions based on direction and an what next to conclude, but to add product related conversational constructs, for a potential sales outcome is required to be crafted between the greeting, introductions based on direction and the what next or conclusion.
