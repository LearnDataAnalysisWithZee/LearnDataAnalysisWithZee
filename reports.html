Operational Training

Agent training commences with conversation scripts, because conversations take place when agents interact with customers regardless of wether or not script constructs are in place, for in order to assist agents during the execution of the quote process strategy. Having access to ordered conversation scripts will allow agents to focus on helping customers, rather than worrying about what words to use or not use from situation to situation. The benefits of conversation scripts suited for each occasion is that the chances of conversions are increased. The first step towards designing robust conversation scripts is to centralise what the conversation will revolve around. 'Creating an customer profile and noting their specific requirements after they develop an interest in a product that is offered'. Given the purpose well defined, making use of the quotes today report becomes an all the more meaningful endaevour and a products in possesion list can be extracted from the collection of the respective reports, namely the quotes today reports.
