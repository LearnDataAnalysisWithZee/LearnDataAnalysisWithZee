Agent training commences with conversation scripts, because conversations take place when agents interact with customers regardless of wether or not script constructs are in place, for in order to assist agents during the execution of the quote process strategy. Having access to ordered conversation scripts will allow agents to focus on helping customers, rather than worrying about what words to use or not use from situation to situation. The benefits of conversation scripts suited for each occasion is that the chances of conversions are increased. The first step towards designing robust conversation scripts is to centralise what the conversation will revolve around. 'Creating an customer profile and noting their specific requirements after they develop an interest in a product that is offered'. Given the purpose well defined, making use of the quotes today report becomes an all the more meaningful endaevour and a products in possesion list can be extracted from the collection of the respective reports, namely the quotes today reports to get started designing conversation scripts for each product.

With the idea well underway and an conversation script for each product offered now created in the agent toolbox, one cannot fail to miss how each script well organised as per product, but how will content that is upto standards fill these conversation scripts? The client query reports are a set of reports that encapsulate day-to-day customer-agent interactions and serves as an excellent corpus for the conversation scripts to consume however what we precisely select from these collections of reports needs to be in accordance with general conversational rules while being compatible with the product specifications, in the context of, 'quoting customers on products offered by a business that they (the customer) have developed an interest in'. General conversational rules are that there is usually a greeting, introductions based on direction and an what next to conclude, but to add product related conversational constructs, for a potential sales outcome is required to be crafted between the greeting, introductions based on direction and the what next or conclusion.
